## Philosophy

My day is structured around the 5 daily prayers. The Islamic day begins at Maghrib, so my 24-hour cycle runs Maghrib → Isha → sleep → Fajr → Zuhr → Asr → Maghrib. How I end the day determines how my morning goes, which determines my entire day.

I am a 2nd-year Computer Science student at KNUST (Year 2, Semester 2). I am also building Pockera (my personal fintech project with Jesse) and contributing to Peasah (a school team project). I create content on social media and write a Substack newsletter about AI and the future of work.

---

## Daily Spine (every day, non-negotiable)

**4:00am** — Wake up. Tahajjud prayer + Quran reading until Fajr.

**~5:00–5:10am** — Fajr prayer time. Walk to the mosque. This walk is my first physical movement of the day.

**~5:30am** — Workout (pushups + jogging). Light workout. If Fajr ran late, this shifts accordingly. On days with lectures ending at 7pm, skip evening workout — morning only.

**~6:00am** — Dhuha prayer (must happen before 10am). Then grooming + breakfast.

**~6:15am** — Call Mum and Dad. Every day.

**~6:30am** — Deep work begins. (See daily schedule below.)

**Zuhr (~12:30pm)** — Stop work. Pray. Reflect on the morning. Think about what the afternoon needs.

**Asr (~4:30pm)** — Wrap up whatever I am doing. Signals the day is ending.

**Maghrib (~6:15pm)** — Day officially ends. Stop all work, school, and hobbies. No exceptions except Wednesday (lecture ends 7pm — makeup Maghrib at home ~7:30pm).

**Post-Maghrib** — Wind down. YouTube, reading Substack articles, chatting friends, checking email. Screens are fine here.

**Isha (~8:00pm)** — Prepare for bed. Evening grooming. Switch to offline: read a physical book.

**9:00pm** — Bed. (Wednesday exception: 10:00pm due to late lectures.)

**Fasting** — Every Monday and Thursday. Suhoor ends at Fajr. Iftar at Maghrib. Light workouts maintained on these days.

## Day Colour System

**Green** — Normal day. Everything progressing as planned.

**Yellow** — Minor disruption. PA adjusts by cutting lowest-priority tasks first. Priority stack:
1. Spine (prayers, sleep, physical) — never cut
2. Lectures — never cut for minor reasons
3. School work (Peasah and assigned work > revision)
4. Hobbies (Pockera > content creation)
5. Mental (reading, YouTube, scrolling)
6. Personal (social, calls, emails)

**Red** — Major event (positive or negative) breaks the day. PA clears the schedule and reschedules high-priority items across other days in the week.

## Edge Cases & Protocols

**Late Fajr:** Everything downstream shifts. PA adjusts the day and notifies me.

**Lecture cancelled:**
- Notice at 9–11am for a 1pm class → extend deep work session
- Notice for a 3pm/5pm class → use the time for more work or wind-down activities
- PA should always ask what I want to do with the reclaimed time

**Wednesday (ends 7pm):** No evening workout. Maghrib makeup at home. Bedtime 10pm. Treat as a protected lecture day — minimal expectations outside school.

**Fasting days (Mon/Thu):** Energy may dip mid-afternoon. PA should flag this if scheduling demanding tasks in that window. I can override.

**Visiting friends:** If a visit risks eating into my 3 core tasks or my sleep window, PA should flag it and ask how to adjust.

**Sick day:** PA clears my schedule. When I say I'm back, PA decides whether to resume work or continue rest based on how I'm feeling.

**Unplanned social:** Core people are Mum and Dad (daily calls, morning). All other social is unscheduled. PA doesn't prompt for it.

**Scrolling:** PA should be aware I scroll. No scheduling needed — just awareness for context.
